MemorEasy Export Link Collector

Drag the button below to your browser's bookmarks bar. Then open Snapchat's Download My Data page, expand Your exports, and click the bookmark.

Collect Snapchat exports

Keep the resulting file private. Its temporary signed URLs grant access to your Snapchat export until they expire. MemorEasy ignores export_urls.txt in Git.

The collector runs entirely inside the current Snapchat page. It selects only signed HTTPS ZIP links hosted on storage.googleapis.com, removes duplicates, orders the numbered archives, and creates a local text file. It makes no network requests of its own.
